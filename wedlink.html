Wedlink

Reimagining the Wedding Workforce

Ethnographic Research Service Design Platform Design Stakeholder Mapping Figma Prototype
Timeline: 8 weeks Type: UXR & Service Design

Overview

WedLink is a human-centered digital platform designed to address deeply rooted challenges by leveraging technology not just as a transactional tool, but as an infrastructure for dignity, fairness, and trust across the wedding workforce ecosystem.

The Problem

India conducts 8 to 10 million weddings per year, generating one of the world's largest informal economies. Over 10 million people work in the "wedding economy." Yet millions of informal workers face:

  • Delayed and withheld payments
  • No formal contracts
  • Poor working conditions
  • Zero insurance or social protection
  • Exploitation by middlemen

Research

Ethnographic field research across wedding venues, vendor interviews, and secondary data analysis of the unorganized wedding workforce. We immersed ourselves in the ecosystem — speaking with caterers, decorators, logistics staff, photographers, labourers, and event planners.

Stakeholder Ecosystem

Mapping the complex web of planners, vendors, labourers, clients, decorators, caterers, and middlemen that make up the Indian wedding industry.

Key Insights

  • Workers lack bargaining power due to informal employment structures
  • Payment delays are systemic and normalised
  • No standardized contracts exist in the sector
  • Middlemen take disproportionate cuts from workers' earnings
  • Digital literacy and mobile penetration are growing — creating an opportunity

Wedlink Platform

A neutral digital ecosystem connecting, regulating, and protecting vendors and labourers through:

  • Transparent job matching — fair, searchable, skill-based listings
  • Digital escrow payments — funds held securely until work is completed
  • Digital contracts — standardised, readable agreements
  • Rating & review systems — accountability for all parties
  • Crew management tools — for planners to coordinate teams effectively

User Flows

Designed end-to-end user journeys for vendors, labourers, and event planners including onboarding, job discovery, booking, payment, and rating systems.

UI Design

High-fidelity screens designed in Figma covering the core experience: dashboard, job listings, payment tracking, reviews, and crew management.

Solution Framework

Policy: Social security, micro-insurance for gig workers.

Technology: Digital escrow payments, job-matching platform.

Industry: Vendor guilds, fair-work certification standards.

Impact

Wedlink bridges the gap between informal labour and fair work practices, creating accountability and transparency in an industry worth hundreds of billions. By positioning technology as infrastructure for dignity rather than just transactional convenience, Wedlink aspires to be a model for the future of informal gig economies.
